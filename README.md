# Dekkoly

Dekkoly is a simple multiplayer-style adventure game where you can explore the world, customize your character, change the visual theme, open the map, and chat.

How to Start
Open Dekkoly.
On the main screen, click PLAY.
Customize your character:
Enter your name.
Choose your character color.
Select your favorite theme.
Click Start! to enter the game.
Moving Around
On PC

Use the following keys to move:

W — Move up
A — Move left
S — Move down
D — Move right

You can change these controls from Settings.

On Mobile

A virtual joystick appears when playing on a touch device.

Drag the joystick in the direction you want your character to move.

Map

You can view the map in two ways:

Click the 🗺️ button in the top-right corner.
Press H on the keyboard.

You can also click the small map in the top-left corner to open the larger map.

Press the × button to close the large map.

Chat

To open the chat:

Click the 💬 button.
Or press T.

Type your message and press Enter or click the ➤ button to send it.

Changing the Theme

You can change the game's appearance at any time.

During the Game
Click the 🎨 button on mobile.
Press M on PC.
Character Customization

You can also select a theme before starting the game.

Available themes include:

Tokyo Night
Dracula
Monokai
Molokai
Henna
Oceanic
Settings

From the main menu, select Settings to configure:

Control mode
Movement keys
Theme shortcut
Map shortcut
Chat shortcut

The control mode can be set to:

Auto — Automatically detects your device.
Keyboard — Uses keyboard controls.
Touch — Uses the virtual joystick.
Returning to the Menu

While playing:

Click the ☰ button in the top-right corner.
Or press Esc.

If a map or chat window is open, pressing Esc closes it first.

Tips
Customize your name and color before starting.
Try the different themes to find your favorite style.
Use the minimap to see your position in the world.
The large map is useful for getting an overview of the entire world.
On mobile, use the joystick for movement and the buttons on the screen for other actions.
